# Site: bruine.dev landing, docs, compare, changelog

Scope: `site/` (Next.js, static export), routes `/[lang]/`, `/[lang]/docs/`, `/[lang]/compare/`, `/[lang]/changelog/`, lang `en` (default) and `fr`.
Mode: Persuade (home, compare), Read (docs, changelog).

Audience: terminal developers; equally those who run their own model and those coming from Claude Code / Codex / OpenCode. Action: copy the install command. Proof: the real recorded session, the stills, the film. Constraints: no invented users, numbers or claims; say what bruine does not do.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is one bruine session: it runs on its own in the first screen and plays again, chapter by chapter, as you scroll. Composition follows the user's pinned reference, deepseek.com/en/harness: centred offer over a night sky, the product window below it, a 2x2 grid of capability cards each shown by the real terminal, a developers block, a centred closing.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground #0b0d14 with faint pastel rain; terminal window #11141e edged #262c3f; sky #7dcfff, lavender #b4a7ff, pink #ff9ed2, mint #8fe3a3, amber #f2cf73 reserved for asking. JetBrains Mono sets display lines and every terminal; Inter sets prose. Frames are the TUI's own rounded box-drawing frames.

STORY: The visitor sees bruine ask to install itself, scrolls through a real session (prompt, reasoning, queue, approval, retry, image, light theme, the weather), sees the film, reads what it does not do, and copies the command.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Under a full-width night sky with lit clouds: the wordmark, the one-line offer in mono at display size, the lead, one light pill button (Install bruine, to the amber band below), then the terminal window at up to 1040px wide autoplaying the recorded session. The amber approval band follows directly under the window.

FORM: Scroll-pinned live session (structure 4 of 7 on the ordered list, chosen by the user), seed c2aea1b5. Signature interaction: the pinned terminal scrubs the real recording frame by frame with scroll; the approval band answers y/a/n keys. Motion grammar: the scrub, the rain whose intensity follows the weather chapter, one ring ripple when a command is copied.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
